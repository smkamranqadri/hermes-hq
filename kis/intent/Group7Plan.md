Facts that shape it: Hermes cron jobs fire from the **gateway ticker** (`hermes cron status`) — specialists are idle-stopped by hq, so hq ticks their cron itself; `hermes cron tick` runs due jobs once and exits; server clock is UTC; hq's venv gains `croniter` (Hermes pins 6.0.0); `zoneinfo` is stdlib.

Shared rules: firing happens in the dispatcher tick (20 s) even while dispatching is paused (a creation is a write, not a claim), never in `--no-dispatcher` mode; missed windows catch up **at most once** per schedule ("late"); cron/`hermes cron tick` subprocesses go through `backend/jobs.py`/allow-listed env; nothing here deletes the paused legacy WM crons (`dfe30ff9e8bf`, `040334fe79ae`, `b84db989076d`).
